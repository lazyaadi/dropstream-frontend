Place a short chime MP3 at `chime.mp3` in this folder.

Recommended:
- File: chime.mp3
- Format: MPEG audio, 16–64 KB, mono, 22050–44100 Hz
- Purpose: Short (100–300ms) chime for notifications

Example path served to client: `/sounds/chime.mp3`

If you want a free chime, consider generating or downloading a CC0 short chime and saving it here as `chime.mp3`.
